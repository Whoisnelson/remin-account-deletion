Delete your Remin account

We're sorry to see you go. If you would like to permanently delete your Remin account and associated data, you can submit a deletion request using the instructions below.

How to delete your account

You can delete your Remin account directly from the Remin app.

1

Open Remin

Open the Remin app and make sure you are signed in.

2

Open Account Settings

Navigate to your account settings from your profile.

3

Select "Delete account"

Follow the confirmation steps and enter your password to permanently delete your account.

What happens when you delete your account?

When your account is deleted, Remin will delete personal information and content associated with your account, including:

  • Your Remin account and authentication information
  • Your profile information
  • Your posts and comments
  • Your followers and following information
  • Other account data associated with your Remin profile

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once the deletion process has been completed.

Can't access your account?

If you are unable to access your Remin account or cannot delete your account through the app, you can contact us and request account deletion.

Contact Remin Support

Data retention

Remin does not retain personal account information after an account deletion request has been successfully processed, except where retention is required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Any data retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as necessary to satisfy the applicable requirement.
